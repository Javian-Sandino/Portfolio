01 — About

Curious about people,
obsessed with the brain.

I'm a CS student at the University of Florida minoring in Economics and Digital Arts & Sciences — which is a formal way of saying I like understanding how systems work, whether they're markets, machines, or minds.

What pulls me in most is neurotechnology. Right now I'm a BCI researcher decoding real-time EEG signals so people can control drones with nothing but focus. There's something magical about turning a thought into motion — it's the closest thing to wizardry engineering has to offer.

Outside the lab, I'm happiest talking to people. I genuinely love conversations — understanding what makes someone tick is its own kind of signal processing. And when I'm not doing that, I'm probably making or listening to music, the original brain-computer interface.
