WHO'S TALKING

I'm a CS student at the University of Florida, minoring in Economics and Digital Arts & Sciences — which is a formal way of saying I like understanding how systems work, whether they're markets, machines, or minds.

The thing that pulls me in hardest is neurotechnology. Right now I decode real-time EEG so people can fly drones with nothing but focus. Turning a thought into motion is the closest thing engineering has to wizardry.

But my favorite interface is still a good conversation. Understanding what makes someone tick is its own kind of signal processing — and when I'm not doing that, I'm behind the decks or on the court.
