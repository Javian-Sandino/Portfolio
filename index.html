01 About

Curious about people.
Obsessed with the brain.

I'm a CS student at the University of Florida minoring in Economics and Digital Arts & Sciences — a formal way of saying I like understanding how systems work, whether they're markets, machines, or minds.

What pulls me in most is neurotechnology. As a BCI researcher I decode real-time EEG signals so people can fly drones with nothing but focus. Turning a thought into motion is the closest thing engineering has to wizardry.

Off the clock I'm behind a pair of decks or on a basketball court. I love talking to people and figuring out what makes them tick — reading a crowd, reading a defense, reading a signal. It's all pattern recognition.
